Reactivity & Reactions:
Reacts violently with cold water to form Sodium Hydroxide (NaOH) and Hydrogen gas (2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂↑). Stored under kerosene oil.
Real-World Applications:
Used in sodium-vapor street lamps, table salt (NaCl), baking soda (NaHCO₃), and as a liquid coolant in fast-breeder nuclear reactors.
Discovery & Historical Note:
Isolated in 1807 by Sir Humphry Davy via electrolysis of molten caustic soda (NaOH).
